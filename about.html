Our Story

Why Abhidheya Advisors exists.

Abhidheya Advisors LLP was founded in 2025 with a clear conviction — that effective consulting in Himalayan states requires more than frameworks imported from elsewhere. It requires practitioners who are embedded in the terrain, who understand the institutional culture of mountain governance, and who stay accountable to outcomes rather than deliverables.

The firm was founded by Vinit Chakwal following years of frontline work in public policy, tourism, skilling, and governance across Uttarakhand — including as a Mahatma Gandhi National Fellow in Pauri Garhwal and as a Strategy Consultant embedded within a state organisation. Abhidheya Advisors was built to take that experience and deploy it at scale — as a structured consulting firm with the agility of a small team and the range of a multi-sectoral practice.

Today the firm operates from Dehradun with active projects in Uttarakhand — and a growing portfolio spanning governance, tourism, digital transformation, communications, and development sector consulting.

How We Work

Four stages.
One continuous loop.

Every engagement follows the same discipline — grounded in research, shaped into strategy, carried through implementation, and closed with measurement. No shortcuts at any stage.

01
Research

Ground-up evidence gathering, field studies, stakeholder consultations, and institutional mapping to understand the real problem before designing any solution.

02
Strategy

Theory of change, programme design, policy frameworks, and resource strategies shaped by evidence and tested against implementation feasibility.

03
Implementation

Embedded support, PMU/TSU deployment, stakeholder coordination, and process management — staying present and accountable through execution.

04
M&E

Real-time monitoring, outcome documentation, impact reporting, and programme learning — closing the loop between design and delivery.

The Founder

Vinit Chakwal

Vinit Chakwal is the Designated Partner of Abhidheya Advisors LLP. He holds a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication from GB Pant University of Agriculture & Technology and a Certificate in Public Policy & Management from IIM Bangalore.

His career spans co-founding Abhidheya Foundation — a youth-driven NGO working with over 1,500 children in Dehradun — serving as a Mahatma Gandhi National Fellow in Pauri Garhwal, and working as a Strategy Consultant with state organisations. He founded Abhidheya Advisors LLP in 2025.

His work sits at the intersection of governance, digital transformation, tourism, and strategic planning — with a particular focus on Uttarakhand's institutional landscape and its development challenges.

What We Stand For

Five principles that guide
every engagement.

Embedded over advisory

We work inside institutions, not at arm's length. Our best work happens when we are present on the ground — not sending reports from a distance.

Evidence before prescription

Every strategy we design is grounded in research. We do not arrive with templates. We listen, study, and then design — in that order.

Accountability to outcomes

We measure ourselves by what changes, not what we deliver. Deliverables are means, not ends. The question is always — did it work?

Systems architects

We don't just solve problems. We build systems, processes, and institutions that keep delivering long after the engagement ends.

Uttarakhand first

We are not a Delhi firm that works occasionally in the hills. This state is our primary context, our laboratory, and our commitment. Everything we do is shaped by a deep understanding of Uttarakhand's institutional landscape, its ecology, and its people.
